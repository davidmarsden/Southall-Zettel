Council end-of-term progress report on the 2022–26 housing commitment. It records 1,779 genuinely affordable homes started since May 2022, a further 909 in the pipeline, and the June 2025 purchase of 180 homes from Berkeley Group at the Green Quarter for affordable housing, including social rent and London Living Rent homes.
