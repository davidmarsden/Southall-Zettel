Primary council scrutiny report defining Ealing's genuinely affordable homes measure and the transition from the 2018–22 target of 2,500 to the 2022–26 target of 4,000. It states that 2,576 homes were counted as delivered in the earlier programme and makes explicit that the measure includes both starts and completions.
