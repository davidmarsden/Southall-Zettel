Berkeley Homes' formal representation on Ealing's Community Infrastructure Levy. It identifies Berkeley as developer of the Southall Gasworks / Green Quarter site, records the revised masterplan and its capacity for 8,100 homes across the site, and states that financial contributions towards local facilities and services were to be secured through the Section 106 agreement.
