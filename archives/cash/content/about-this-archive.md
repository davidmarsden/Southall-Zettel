This is an **unofficial preserved copy** of the website of Clean Air for Southall and Hayes (CASH), the community campaign group formed in 2018 over pollution and public-health concerns linked to the redevelopment of the former Southall Gasworks (Southall Waterside).

It is kept by [Southall Stories](https://southallstories.uk) as part of a public-interest research archive. **It is not run by, affiliated with or endorsed by CASH.** All campaign text, testimonies and images remain the work of CASH and its contributors.

## Why it exists

CASH's website was at `southallandhayescleanair.org.uk`. That domain is no longer controlled by the campaign and now serves unrelated spam content. **Do not visit it, and do not cite it as a source for CASH.**

The campaign's public record of the Southall Gasworks controversy, including residents' testimonies, its demands and the documents it published, has been reconstructed here from the Internet Archive so it stays readable and citable.

## How it was made

- Every page was rebuilt from snapshots held by the [Internet Archive's Wayback Machine](https://web.archive.org/web/2022/https://southallandhayescleanair.org.uk/), using the latest capture made **before** the domain changed hands (captures from August 2020 to May 2022).
- Text is reproduced **as published**, including original spelling. Navigation, layout and design are new.
- The three PDFs CASH hosted are given as full-text transcriptions, each linking to its archived original.
- Photographs were recovered byte-for-byte from the Wayback Machine and are stored with the archive.
- Videos embedded on the original pages are still hosted on YouTube by their original channels. They are shown here with YouTube’s privacy-enhanced player, which loads only when you press play.
- Each page shows its original address, capture date and a link to the archived snapshot, so anything here can be checked against the source.
- Left out: the Contact page (the archive could not return it), a default WordPress "Hello world!" placeholder post, and private phone numbers printed on the Save Our Southall leaflet.

## Beyond the website

The [Beyond the website](elsewhere.html) section adds CASH material and official records found elsewhere online: the campaign’s petitions, crowdfunding page and letters, Ealing Council correspondence and legal advice, and GLA and parliamentary records. Each preserved text gives its source address and retrieval date.

If you are connected with CASH and want something corrected, credited differently or removed, please contact Southall Stories via [southallstories.uk](https://southallstories.uk).
