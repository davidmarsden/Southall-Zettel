The CASH website told only part of the story. This section gathers material from elsewhere on the web, so the record survives if the original pages disappear:

- the campaign’s own petitions, letters and crowdfunding appeal;
- official records of how Ealing Council, the Greater London Authority and Parliament responded;
- reports, videos and press coverage.

<p class="note">
<strong>What is preserved in full, and what is not.</strong>
Material written by CASH and its founders, and public-sector records (published under the Open Government Licence or Open Parliament Licence), is reproduced in full on this site.
Journalism and other people’s work is listed with a short neutral summary and links to the original and to any Wayback Machine copy, but is not reproduced.
Phone numbers and email addresses in letterheads have been left out.
Everything was retrieved on 8 November 2026.
</p>
