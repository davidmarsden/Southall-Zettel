Parliamentary record · 2021-01-21

Air Pollution: Brownfield Redevelopment — House of Commons, Oral Answers (Environment, Food and Rural Affairs), 21 January 2021

The Southall MP (speaker names not in the extraction; almost certainly Virendra Sharma) says constituents 'feel choked' by gasworks remediation fumes and asks why the EA and PHE are not empowered to act. The minister replies it is a matter for the local authority and the Mayor.

Source: https://hansard.parliament.uk/commons/2021-01-21/debates/0476D713-A47A-4E95-96DD-AC57B4B64B76/AirPollutionBrownfieldRedevelopment (live, fetched 2026-11-08). Hansard Volume 687. Open Parliament Licence. Speaker names were not present in the text extraction; the questioner refers to "my constituency" and Southall gasworks, so is almost certainly Virendra Sharma MP (Ealing, Southall); the answering minister is not identified in the extraction (unverified — likely a Defra minister). CASH is not named.

Question (911169): What recent discussions he has had with Cabinet colleagues on the level of air pollution generated from the redevelopment of brownfield sites.

Answer: The Secretary of State regularly meets Cabinet colleagues to discuss a range of issues, including air quality and planning issues. Air quality is a key consideration of local authority planning decisions, and there are strong protections in place to safeguard people from unacceptable risks from air pollution where development is proposed, and this is detailed in national planning policy.

Supplementary: People living in my constituency feel choked by the fumes from the remediation of Southall gasworks, a project forced through against the wishes of local residents and local representatives by the then Mayor of London, Boris Johnson. Will the Minister confirm that they will meet me and local residents to explain why they think this is acceptable and why the Government will not empower either the Environment Agency or Public Health England to act?

Answer: I thank the hon. Gentleman for his question. The redevelopment of the former gasworks site at Southall is a matter for the local authority and the Mayor of London, as I am sure he is well aware. Local authorities are required to review and assess local air quality and decide what monitoring is necessary in line with statutory duties. This Government are tackling air quality and taking it extremely seriously with their £3.8 billion project. If the hon. Gentleman wants to contact me with any details about this issue, I am happy to speak to him but I am not able to get involved in any way in particular planning issues.
