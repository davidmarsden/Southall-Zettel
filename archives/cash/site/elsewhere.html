Gathered from across the web

Beyond the website

CASH material and records about the campaign that never appeared on its website, gathered from across the web and preserved.

The CASH website told only part of the story. This section gathers material from elsewhere on the web, so the record survives if the original pages disappear:

  • the campaign’s own petitions, letters and crowdfunding appeal;
  • official records of how Ealing Council, the Greater London Authority and Parliament responded;
  • reports, videos and press coverage.

What is preserved in full, and what is not. Material written by CASH and its founders, and public-sector records (published under the Open Government Licence or Open Parliament Licence), is reproduced in full on this site. Journalism and other people’s work is listed with a short neutral summary and links to the original and to any Wayback Machine copy, but is not reproduced. Phone numbers and email addresses in letterheads have been left out. Everything was retrieved on 8 November 2026.

  • Change.org · 8 July 2018

    Clean Air for Southall and Hayes! Stop the contaminated old gasworks polluting our kids! (Change.org petition)

    Founding petition of the campaign, started by resident David Marsden, demanding Berkeley Group install effective odour controls and halt the on-site 'soil hospital'; names Ealing Council, the EA, MP Virendra Sharma and Berkeley staff as unresponsive. Closed with 918 supporters.

    Change.org petition updates, 30 Jul 2018 (letter to Simon Dawkins, EA) and 12 Aug 2018 (public meeting at Roxy's)

    Two petition updates by David Marsden: an open letter asking the Environment Agency officer visiting the site to force a halt to soil-hospital operations during the 2018 heatwave, and a report of door-knocking on Beaconsfield Road plus notice of a public meeting on 13 Aug 2018.

  • CrowdJustice · 31 July 2019

    Stop Poisoning the Southall Community! (CrowdJustice case page, incl. Updates 1-2)

    CASH's crowdfunding page to instruct environmental lawyers (Clyde & Co listed) for a public-nuisance claim against Berkeley Group, possibly also Ealing Council and the Environment Agency. Funded 31 July 2019; GBP 6,222 from 94 pledges against a GBP 10,000 stretch target (case text says minimum GBP 25,000). Two updates (24 Sep 2019: joint CASH/Extinction Rebellion die-in at Ealing Town Hall on 14 Sep 2019; 15 Oct 2019: Daily Mirror coverage).

    Read preserved textOriginalWayback

  • Ealing Council (hosting CASH letter) · 22 July 2022

    Open letter from CASH to Cllr Peter Mason (22 Jul 2022) and Leader's reply (2 Nov 2022)

    CASH's open letter, signed by Angela Fonso after its 'Air Pollution in Southall is a Health Emergency' webinar, says the site air monitors are 'not fit for purpose' (no VOC monitoring, badly sited), cites Brunel soil findings on lead/copper, and lists demands. The council reply lists actions taken since 2021 and rejects most of the demands, saying an abatement notice would 'certainly fail'.

  • UK Parliament, Environmental Audit Committee · Undated

    Written evidence AIR0008 — AGHAST (Marie Sansford) to Environmental Audit Committee Air Pollution Inquiry

    Brighton gasworks campaign's submission cites Southall as a cautionary case. It says Berkeley Group acknowledged to AGHAST in August 2023 that siting the Southall soil hospital against garden walls was 'a mistake'. It also says Southall residents were told the smells came from their own detergents or smoking.

    Berkeley Group acknowledged to AGHAST in August 2023 that this was a mistake and said they are still learning how to best decontaminate ex-gasworks sites.

  • Ealing Council (moderngov) · 29 July 2019

    Southall Broadway Ward Forum 29 July 2019 — Air quality in Southall, concerns about health and the odour

    Council briefing summarising PHE's interim health risk assessment (v3, 9 July 2019) of Atkins VOC data from Jan 2018 to May 2019. It says the soil hospital closed in March 2019, that there is 'unlikely to be a direct toxicological risk', and that the EA's odour recommendations had been met by the developer.

  • Ealing Council (counsel: Stuart Jessop, 6 Pump Court) · 19 June 2020

    Advice in respect of the Southall Waterside development and the F.M. Conway site (counsel's opinion, Stuart Jessop)

    Counsel's opinion to Ealing on its statutory-nuisance duties. It names CASH as alleging odour nuisance and records that CASH commissioned the Centric Lab report, which counsel criticises (sample of 10) while recommending a fresh PHE report that addresses it. It concludes the council was entitled to rely on PHE 2019 and to find no statutory nuisance.

  • Hansard, UK Parliament · 21 January 2021

    Air Pollution: Brownfield Redevelopment (Commons oral question, Defra)

    The Southall MP (speaker names not in the extraction; almost certainly Virendra Sharma) says constituents 'feel choked' by gasworks remediation fumes and asks why the EA and PHE are not empowered to act. The minister replies it is a matter for the local authority and the Mayor.

  • Ealing Council · 19 May 2021

    Letter from Cllr Peter Mason (new Leader) to CASH setting out seven-point plan

    The day after he became Leader, Mason told CASH the council had too often seemed to be 'batting for the developers'. He instructed officers to commission legal advice on an abatement notice, involve CASH in independent air monitoring, work with Centric Lab, review Berkeley sponsorship and lobby government.

    Too often, it has seemed – rightly or wrongly – like the council is batting for the developers and not for residents.

    Read preserved textOriginal

  • Ealing Council (counsel: Stuart Jessop) · 20 July 2021

    Updated counsel's opinion on Southall Waterside / FM Conway (Stuart Jessop), July 2021

    Second opinion commissioned under Mason's plan. It again finds no evidence of statutory nuisance or prejudice to health on the PHE 2019 and 2020 reports. It flags a mismatch between residents' strength of feeling and the very low complaint numbers (two in 2021) and suggests smell diaries. It notes that CASH or residents could bring their own s.82 EPA proceedings, and puts the cost of a contested abatement notice well above GBP 100k.

    Read preserved textOriginal

    Letter from Cllr Peter Mason to CASH re Citizens Tribunal

    Short reply to Angela Fonso and CASH saying the council's Citizens Tribunal is independent and the Leader will not direct what it looks at. It follows a 2 Aug 2023 letter on Berkeley Group sponsorship of council events.

  • Ealing Council · 28 September 2023

    Cllr Peter Mason reply to Zack Polanski AM (Warren Farm; Southall Gasworks & CASH)

    Replying to an open letter from the Green AM, Mason restates the seven-point plan and says an abatement notice would lead to 'almost certain failure' in court. He says the council could not have shown a statutory nuisance even when odours were at their worst during soil-hospital operations.

    the Council would not have been able to demonstrate that a statutory nuisance had occurred, even when the foul smells from the remediation of soil at the Gasworks site were at their most acute

Reports 2

  • Centric Lab · 13 May 2020

    Air Pollution & Health in Southall, London (Centric Lab report, co-produced with CASH)

    Centric Lab's three-month pro-bono study with CASH argues that the guidelines PHE used did not account for the Southall community's susceptibility (chronic physical and psychosocial stressors). It calls for stricter guidelines and a cross-sectional health study.

    In the following co-produced report with Clean Air for Southall & Hayes, we put gathering evidence to highlight the susceptibility of the Southall community and the need for stricter air pollution guidelines.

    Read preserved textOriginal

  • National Retrofit Hub · 24 June 2026

    Community Health Impact Assessment: Centric Lab (CHIA scenario case study)

    Case study of the community health impact assessment (CHIA) template co-developed by Centric Lab and CASH from 2022. It says the template is now a toolkit and learning programme documented in peer-reviewed journals.

    “What if the HIA (Health Impact Assessment( was designed by people like us, would the approaches have been different; would we have fallen so ill?”

Press coverage 12

  • The Observer / Guardian · 4 May 2019

    Families hit out at London gasworks redevelopment

    Early Observer report on parents' claims that weak pollution regulation at the Southall brownfield site endangers children. AGHAST's evidence cites it as reporting high naphthalene and an HSE demand for action.

    Original

  • MyLondon (Ged Cann, Local Democracy Reporter) · 20 May 2019

    'We demand to be heard over Southall Waterside health concerns'

    Residents, including Angela Fonso of CASH, criticise Ealing for postponing a promised public meeting until after the EU elections because of purdah rules. The report details PHE findings that naphthalene was regularly above guideline values at the site boundary.

    Mrs Fonso is part of Clean Air for Southall and Hayes (CASH) and said residents wanted a public meeting with “people with decision making powers”.

    Original

  • The Observer / Guardian (Jo Griffin) · 7 July 2019

    Residents prepare court challenge over redevelopment of London gasworks site

    Reports the campaign's planned public-nuisance action, fronted by Jo Sidhu QC, ahead of the 10 July 2019 public meeting. It quotes PHE that naphthalene 'should urgently be reduced' and notes Fonso's demand for independent monitoring and removal of a Berkeley manager as a school governor.

    “We will demand that there is independent air quality monitoring of the site and steps to reduce naphthalene levels,” said Angela Fonso, from the local campaign group.

    Original

    Trade-press report on campaigners' health claims about the development.

  • Ealing Matters · 23 August 2019

    Southall Gasworks Meeting: Where is Council Support for Residents?

    Account of the 10 July 2019 public meeting at the Dominion Centre (200+ residents) with council, Berkeley, EA and PHE, chaired by Peter Mason and opened by Julian Bell. It describes broken promises and an angry meeting that ended with no next steps, and notes CASH's crowdfunded legal plans.

    The community group Clean Air for Southall and Hayes (CASH) is now planning to take the developer and Ealing Council to court and have launched a crowd-funding campaign to help with legal costs.

    Original

  • Daily Mirror (Ros Wynne-Jones, Real Britain) · 11 October 2019

    Boris Johnson's toxic legacy means residents think they're being gassed at front door

    National feature on residents' illnesses near Southall Waterside, framing the development as Johnson's 2010 mayoral call-in. It reports that CASH, founded by Angela Fonso, is preparing legal action with support from Jo Sidhu QC, and carries council and Berkeley responses.

    Local mum Angela Fonso, 51, founded CASH. “Depending on the direction of the wind, some days are worse than others,” she says.

  • The Guardian (Jo Griffin, Maeve Shearlaw, Kyri Evangelou) · 27 August 2020

    Londoners claim toxic air from gasworks damaging their health

    Year-long Guardian investigation following CASH. Of 48 residents returning questionnaires, all reported breathing problems. Prof Roy Harrison said naphthalene data 'well exceeded' PHE guidelines, and the piece covers Centric Lab's susceptibility report.

    “I don’t believe this environmental injustice would happen in a more affluent white area.”

    Original

  • The Guardian (Jo Griffin) · 22 September 2020

    Residents demand new clean air rules for former gasworks sites in England

    Launch of Gasworks Communities United, an alliance of residents near the Southall, Hornsey, Lea Bridge and east Brighton gasworks sites demanding independent assessment of remediation. Angela Fonso is quoted as a GCU member and Southall coordinator, and Siân Berry comments.

    “Our experience in Southall shows there must be independent assessment of remediation strategies, better scrutiny by the local council and more accountability to residents,” said Angela Fonso

  • The Guardian (Sammy Gecsoyler) · 27 April 2023

    Scientists to examine health fears at west London luxury development

    Imperial College (Dr Ian Mudway) biomonitoring study, funded via the NIHR unit for chemical and radiation hazards, will deploy about 50 samplers and collect blood and urine from Southall residents. Fonso, described as CASH coordinator, welcomes it. Notes the APPG on gasworks redevelopment chaired by Virendra Sharma has disbanded.

    “This research project is a massive step forward for us. At last we might start to uncover why so many residents are facing these health issues.”
