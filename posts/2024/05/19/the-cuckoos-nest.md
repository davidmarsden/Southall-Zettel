Ealing Genocide Supporters Club (aka Ealing Labour Party) held their Southall Branch meeting yesterday at the Dominion Centre in Southall. Under the guise of a "Your Town, Your Voice" community get together, our local elected repellents gathered on masse in all their finery. I couldn't help myself as they posed for a group photo all gurning inanely as the photographer encouraged them to "say cheese!". "Say genocide!" I offered. "Genocide supporters!"

<img src="https://southallstories.uk/uploads/2025/1dd102187e.jpg" width="600" height="450" alt="Auto-generated description: A group of seven people, dressed in formal and casual attire, are standing together in an indoor setting, smiling for the camera.">

Cllr Dr Murtaza of their number aggressively reprimanded me. "What evidence do you have that we support genocide?" he demanded to know. Well, I told him, my main piece of evidence would be that you have done nothing to oppose it.


Ooh! There's Cllr Martin! Our locally elected anti-Traveller racist! "What evidence do you have to support that?" Well, [here](https://skwawkbox.org/2022/02/14/party-imposed-london-council-candidate-used-holocaust-language-against-gypsy-roma-travellers/) it is.


Oh, and while you're at it, Cllr Dr Murtaza. Next time you are chauffeuring our glorious leader around in your Porsche, try not to [park in a cycle lane](https://twitter.com/DvdMrsdn/status/1587711673942646785?t=U0luZmPHCxRb9wGAC5Ffkw&s=19), please.


Then there was illegal samosa factory proprietor Cllr ["I own half of Southall"](https://sadealing.blogspot.com/2010_10_03_archive.html?m=1) Anand. 

<img src="https://southallstories.uk/uploads/2025/a514ae903a.jpg" width="451" height="878" alt="Auto-generated description: A newspaper page includes an article about a councillor's illegal samosa factory, a feature on a nursery class under the sea, and an entertainment section asking, Have you got soul?">

I hadn't realised until yesterday quite how visceral my revulsion for these people is. The grand [cuckoo in the nest](https://ealing.nub.news/news/local-news/former-ealing-council-housing-leader-admits-he-39made-a-mistake39-accepting-3000-from-developer-for-cannes-property-conference) arrived about half way through the event. I would have confronted him myself, and previously I have done. Maybe I was just worn down from a week with the 'flu, but I felt like I really had to keep my distance for my own sanity. In any case, a few council officers made beelines for me and made sure I was busy answering their questions.

<img src="https://southallstories.uk/uploads/2025/c0c533c73a.jpg" width="600" height="450" alt="Auto-generated description: A display board features information about Your Voice, Your Town, highlighting local community involvement and communication preferences in Ealing, with a photo of a person interacting with sticky notes.">

Interestingly, one officer suggested to me that resident-led ward forums would be the likely outcome of this event. Fantastic news, if that's the case. Another officer I spoke to later, knew nothing about this idea, though, but took copious notes. A neighbour and friend told me that she spoke to the cuckoo himself who told her that this meeting was in fact the replacement for he old (councillor-led) ward forums. That's that then.


The event itself was a repeat of several [resident](https://web.archive.org/web/20040615012717/http://www.southallgasworks.com/Media/ConsultationReport.pdf)[pdf] [surveys](https://www.ealing.gov.uk/downloads/download/6838/southall_shaping_ealing_summary) and [failed](https://www.visitsouthall.co.uk/News/NewsDetails.php?recordID=1006) [plans](https://www.ealing.gov.uk/downloads/download/6999/southall_reset_%E2%80%93_spatial_planning_framework) over the past twenty years or so (for which I've seen records, or taken part in). The problems are always the same. The responses from the council are always the same. Nothing.
