    <p>Last Wednesday, I and two other #Southall residents met with @berkeleygroupuk's @damian_leydon, @rickwatler and @SangheraJags, and their PR company representative and note taker @chattycoley of @LocalDialogue, at their #SouthallWaterside HQ.</p>
    <p>I have to say, I was mightily impressed by their palatial portacabin HQ. The reception area was bigger than my living room, and despite the lack of natural light and windows, the air conditioning was a great relief from the high 20s temperatures outside!</p>
    <p>I'll add more later when I have time. <br>
<br>
Suffice to say for now, we were lied to, and treated disrespectfully, in a cynical attempt to control the agenda of the meeting, and spin their marketing story.</p>
    <p>Forgot to add, Jonathan Houghton, an Environmental Engineer at @atkinsglobal was also present at the meeting.<br>
<br>
David Brent-like, @damian_leydon likes to refer to him as his 'scientist', as if that magically makes everything all right.</p>
