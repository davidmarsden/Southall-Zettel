Southall clean-air campaigner and co-founder of Clean Air for Southall and Hayes (CASH), established in 2018 to campaign around pollution and public-health concerns linked to redevelopment of the former Southall Gasworks. She also stood for Ealing Community Independents in Southall Broadway in the 2026 council elections.
