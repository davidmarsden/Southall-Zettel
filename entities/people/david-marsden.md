Southall-based investigative journalist, publisher of Southall Stories and community campaigner whose work focuses on local democracy, planning, air pollution, public services and civic accountability in the London Borough of Ealing. He stood as an independent candidate for Southall Green ward in the 2022 Ealing Council elections.
