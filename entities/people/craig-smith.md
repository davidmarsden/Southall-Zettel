Ealing community campaigner, trade unionist and founder of Ealing Community Independents. He is a founder of the Save Ealing Children's Centres campaign, an officer of Ealing Trades Union Council, a former school governor and former business journalist who now works in strategic communications. He stood for Ealing Community Independents in the 2026 council elections and the June 2026 North Acton by-election.
