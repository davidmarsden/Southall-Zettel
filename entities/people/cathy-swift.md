Ealing environmental campaigner and Chair of LAGER Can, the Litter Action Group for Ealing Residents. She has led the group's borough-wide growth and campaigning on litter, fly-tipping and waste policy, and stood for Ealing Community Independents in Greenford Broadway at the 2026 council elections.
