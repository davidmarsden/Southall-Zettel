Church of England priest and interfaith practitioner with long-standing roots in Southall. He served for 11 years as Vicar of St John's Southall, later served as Secretary for Inter-Religious Affairs to the Archbishop of Canterbury and National Inter-Religious Affairs Adviser for the Church of England, and continues to support mission, community and interfaith work in Southall. He is also a director of The Kings Centre Southall.
