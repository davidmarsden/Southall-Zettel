Green Party councillor for Hanwell Broadway, elected in May 2026. She previously worked in Ealing Council Children's Services and became a prominent campaigner with Save Ealing Children's Centres, including giving evidence in the 2026 High Court challenge to the council's children's-centre closure programme. She serves on Ealing Council's Children's Services Scrutiny Panel.
