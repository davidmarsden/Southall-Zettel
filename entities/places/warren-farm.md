Large rewilded meadow and open-space site in Norwood Green, Ealing, forming part of Brent River Park and supporting significant grassland and wildlife habitat. The campaigning name `Warren Farm Nature Reserve` refers to the nature-rich site while the graph keeps the broader place identity as Warren Farm.
